Our Story

At Seth's Meat, we believe that great BBQ starts with great ingredients. For over 15 years, we've been perfecting our craft, smoking our brisket low and slow for 14 hours to achieve that perfect bark and tender interior.

We source only the finest cuts from local farms that share our commitment to quality and sustainability. Every piece of meat is seasoned with our secret rub and smoked over real hickory wood.
